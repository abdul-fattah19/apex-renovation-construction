Our Story

Built by referrals.

Apex started as a small team of tradespeople who got tired of the usual builder experience — vague quotes, missed calls, jobs that stall halfway through.

So we started doing things properly: fixed-price quotes agreed before we start, one project manager per job, and every trade on our own team.

Most of our work today still comes from client referrals. That's the best sign we know that a job was done right.
