About

A small team of builders, based in Ilford.

We started Apex because we got tired of how most builders work — vague quotes, missed deadlines, and jobs that stall halfway through. So we do it differently.

Every trade is on our own team. Every quote is fixed before we start. Every project has one manager, and they answer the phone.

  • i.
    Fixed prices, agreed up front
    The number in your quote is the number you pay. No provisional sums.
  • ii.
    One project manager per job
    You get one person to call. Not a rotating cast of subcontractors.
  • iii.
    Two-year workmanship guarantee
    If something we did fails, we come back and fix it. £5M insurance on every job.
